    Kishan (কৃষাণ)

    Kishan (কৃষাণ) AI Platform

    National agricultural intelligence: daily DAM market prices, hyper-local weather alerts & Bengali voice assistant.

    terminal · ~/projects/kishan

    ~/projects/kishan $ glow README.md

    Why I built it

    Farmers across Bangladesh lose hard-earned income to price manipulation by middlemen and unpredictable flash weather events. Kishan was engineered to deliver instant price transparency, AI agronomic guidance, and voice-assisted weather alerts directly into the hands of farmers.

    What it does

    • Tracks daily wholesale vs. retail produce rates from the Department of Agricultural Marketing (DAM) with clear trend indicators (📈/📉).
    • Generates hyper-local 7-day weather forecasts with crop-specific "What to do" and "What to avoid" action plans.
    • In-app Bengali Text-to-Speech (TTS) audio engine reading prices and forecasts aloud with one tap.
    • Pluggable SMS emergency gateway for early warning disaster and heavy precipitation alerts.
    • Offline-first local caching and passwordless guest onboarding for zero friction.

    How it's built

    • Full-stack architecture: Flutter Android mobile application paired with a high-throughput Python FastAPI backend.
    • AI-assisted market analysis with Google Gemini API and automated web scrapers.
    • Open-Meteo meteorological API integration and SQLite cache layer.

    "dependencies": ["Flutter", "Dart", "Python", "FastAPI", "Gemini API", "Open-Meteo", "SQLite"]

    E-Commerce AI Sales & Support Agent

    24/7 intelligent Telegram sales representative and customer support agent powered by Gemini AI.

    terminal · ~/projects/ads-fashion-bot

    ~/projects/ads-fashion-bot $ glow README.md

    Why I built it

    Online fashion and retail businesses lose high-intent buyers whenever inquiries go unanswered during off-hours. This agent provides human-like, polite, and persuasive customer service 24/7, turning curious visitors into paying customers without staffing overhead.

    What it does

    • Conversational sales representative: answers questions regarding fabric quality, stock availability, measurements, and pricing.
    • Context-aware product recommendations based on customer preferences using Google Gemini 2.5 Flash.
    • Automated customer checkout workflow: collects phone number, delivery address, and creates confirmed orders in SQLite.
    • Supervisor admin panel: alerts human operators for custom requests and exports order rosters.

    How it's built

    • Asynchronous Python architecture using `python-telegram-bot` and AsyncIO event loops.
    • Multi-turn prompt engineering with robust system guards and empathetic sales personas.
    • Relational database storage with SQLite for persistent conversation context and transaction logs.
